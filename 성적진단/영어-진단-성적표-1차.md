## ✅ 강점 (기본기 살아있음)

- **관계절** — `The man I met yesterday...` 자연스럽게 구사
- **수동태 + 조동사** — `The report has to be issued...`
- **be going to 미래 구문** — `We are going to move to the new office...`

초급 단계에서는 나오기 어려운 구조들이라, 기초는 확실히 잡혀 있습니다.

## ⚠️ 집중 보완 영역 4가지

| # | 영역 | 대표 실수 | 교정 |
|---|------|-----------|------|
| 1 | **전치사** | late **at** the meeting | late **for** the meeting |
| 2 | **조건문 · 가정법** | We will cancel it **when** it rains | **If** it rains |
| 3 | **시제 일관성** | The man I met... **is** the manager | ...**was** the manager |
| 4 | **비교급 · 가정법 과거완료** | **more cheapper**... if we **could have made** | **cheaper**... If we **had booked** |

**세부 설명**

1. **전치사** — late for, arrive at, depend on, in charge of 등. 토익 파트5 단골 출제 포인트.
2. **조건문·가정법** — `if`(만약~면) vs `when`(~할 때)의 뉘앙스 구분, 가정법 과거완료(`if + had p.p. / 주절 could have p.p.`).
3. **시제 일관성** — 주절이 과거면 관계절·종속절도 과거로 일치시키기.
4. **비교급** — `cheaper`, `faster` 형태. `more + -er` 중복 사용 금지.

## 문장별 채점 상세

| 번호 | 내 답안 | 평가 |
|------|---------|------|
| 1 | I drink coffee every morining. | ✓ (`morning` 철자만) |
| 2 | She works at a bank. | ✓ 완벽 |
| 3 | I was late **at** the meeting yesterday. | △ late **for** |
| 4 | We are going to move to the new office next week. | ✓ 완벽 |
| 5 | We will cancel the workshop **when** it rains. | △ **If** it rains |
| 6 | The man I met yesterday **is** the new manager. | △ **was** (과거 일치) |
| 7 | The report has to be **issued** by Friday. | ✓ (제출은 `submitted`가 더 정확) |
| 8 | We could get **more cheapper** tickets if we **could have made** reservation earier. | ✗ If we **had booked** earlier, we **could have gotten cheaper** tickets |
